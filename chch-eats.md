# Christchurch Eats

A running list of restaurants and cafes in Christchurch, for whenever I can't decide where to eat.

⭐ = top pick

## Malaysian

- ⭐ **City Cafe** *(Jason Ui)* — best Malaysian restaurant
  - Nasi goreng — Highly recommended
  - Mee goreng — Highly recommended
  - Hainanese chicken rice — Highly recommended (Best in Chch)
  - Char ho fun — Highly recommended
  - Curry Laksa — OK
- **Old Street Hawkin Road** *(Jason Ui)* — Used to be good but owner changed, not sure of quality anymore
  - Char ho fun — OK
- ⭐ **Adam's Malaysian Noodles** *(Jason Ui)* — Recommended, must-try at least once
- **ILOVEGULA** *(Jason Ui)* — Recommended for brunch
- **Shirley Kitchen** *(Jason Ui)* — Spicy menu is spicy, nasi lemak great. Has a (secret) pre-order menu — check Facebook page to pre-order dishes for catering/events
- **Rasheka Pure Butter** *(others)* — Both meals and catering dishes. Highly recommended by others
- **Chopstick Restaurant** — Recommend
- **Melaka Kopitiam** — Run by Malaysians, mid-tier Malaysian food. Some reviews suggest food is not good
- ⭐ **5 Foot Way** — On Facebook, open once every so often (approx every 3 months). Sells Singaporean chicken rice (rated better than City Cafe) and also highly rated kueh
- **Hot Jack** — Highly recommended
- **Yummy Restaurant** (Riccarton) — Personally don't recommend, flavours too strong for me, but Leanne likes them for that

  - Chicken chop with deep fried onion

  - Deep fried tofu with mince

## Cambodian

- **Diversity** (Riccarton)
  - Pork Pan Fried Dumplings — crowd-favourite starter, served with black vinegar and chilli soy oil
  - Roti with Peanut Sauce — reviewers consistently call the house-made peanut dipping sauce the best they've ever had
  - Cambodian Beef Salad (or Khmer Red Curry) — highlights the restaurant's authentic Khmer heritage
  - Mee Goreng (or Drunken Noodles) — highly-rated, fresh, and flavorful, easy to portion out
  - Extra Special Fried Rice (or Pineapple Fried Rice) — frequently singled out as a standout base dish for a shared table
